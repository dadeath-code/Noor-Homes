Our Story

Founded in 2004, Noor Homes began with a simple vision: to revolutionize the real estate experience by putting clients first. Over the past two decades, we've grown from a small local agency to one of the most trusted names in real estate.

Our success is built on a foundation of integrity, expertise, and genuine care for our clients' needs. We believe that buying or selling a home is more than a transaction—it's a life-changing journey that deserves personalized attention and professional guidance.

Today, Noor Homes is proud to serve thousands of satisfied clients, offering comprehensive real estate services that span residential sales, rentals, property management, and investment consultation.
